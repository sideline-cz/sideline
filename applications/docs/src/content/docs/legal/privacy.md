The controller of your personal data is **Ondřej Maxa**, who operates the hosted instance as an individual. A postal address is available on request through the contacts below.

You can reach us through [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/maxa-ondrej/sideline/discussions), or by the **Report a bug** link in the app's user menu.

This policy covers the hosted instance at [sideline.cz](https://sideline.cz). Sideline's source code is also available under the MIT licence — **if you self-host, you are the controller of your own instance** and this policy does not apply to you.

## 2. What we collect

**From Discord, when you sign in.** We use Discord OAuth with the scopes `identify`, `guilds` and `guilds.join`. We store your Discord user ID, username, display name, server nickname and avatar.

We do **not** request or store your Discord email address.

`guilds.join` lets us add you to a team's Discord server when you accept an invite. We use it for nothing else.

**What you tell us about yourself.** Your name, date of birth, gender, preferred language, jersey number, playing position and self-rated proficiency.

Date of birth and gender are used to place you automatically into the right age and category groups. You can ask a human to review any such assignment.

**What you do in the product.** Team, group and roster membership; RSVPs and attendance; how many RSVPs you have missed; training logs including any notes you write; weekly challenge completions; achievements; poll votes; carpool participation and notes; and calendar subscriptions.

**Your skill rating.** Sideline keeps an Elo-style rating per player, with a full match-by-match history of every adjustment and who submitted it. It is used to balance generated teams.

**Rules Trainer results.** Your attempts, scores and per-question answers. These are tied to your account rather than to a team, so they deliberately stay with you when you join or leave a team.

**Money.** Fees assigned to you, payments recorded against them, reminders sent to you, and — if you are a treasurer — an audit trail of expenses you create or edit.

**Email forwarding, only if your team turns it on.** If your team connects a mailbox, we store the sender address, subject, **full message body** and attachments of every email it receives, and we send that content to an external AI provider to generate a summary.

Please note that email sent to a connected team mailbox may contain personal data about people who have no Sideline account and never agreed to any of this. Think carefully before enabling this feature.

**Technical data.** Session tokens, calendar-feed tokens, Discord OAuth access and refresh tokens, and operational telemetry — performance traces and error reports, including crash reports sent from your browser.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- **To provide the service** — Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract. Your account, team membership, events, RSVPs, finances and the Discord integration.
- **To keep it working and secure** — Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest. Telemetry, error reporting, abuse prevention and audit records.
- **Optional team features** — Art. 6(1)(a), consent given by your team's admin. Email forwarding and AI summarisation.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Discord** — the product is built on Discord. Your membership, roles, channels, event posts and direct messages pass through it and are covered by [Discord's privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **OpenAI** (`api.openai.com`, model `gpt-4.1`) — receives email content for summarisation, only where a team has enabled email forwarding.
- **An inbound-email delivery provider** — receives and forwards email addressed to a connected team mailbox, where enabled.
- **Our hosting provider** — runs our servers, database and monitoring.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists.

Financial records and certain administrative audit entries are retained after account deletion, because an accurate financial history has to stay intact. Where the law allows, we anonymise rather than delete.

**Email bodies and attachments are deleted 90 days after a message has been handled.** The body is emptied and the attachments removed; the summary that was posted, the sender and the subject are kept, so there is still a record of what arrived and what the bot did with it.

A message still waiting on a decision from your team is not purged, however old it is — otherwise the purge would destroy work in progress. If you need something removed sooner than 90 days, ask your team admin or us.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, hand it to you in a portable format, or withdraw consent you have given.

You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, [Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů](https://uoou.gov.cz).

**How to exercise these rights:** contact us through the channels in section 1. We will respond within one month. There is currently no self-service export or delete button — these requests are handled by a person.

Your team admin can remove you from a team, which deletes your team-scoped data. Deleting your Discord account does **not** automatically delete your Sideline account.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

We use strictly necessary storage only: a session token to keep you signed in, and local storage for your language, theme and sidebar preferences.

We use no advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party tracking.

Our own error and performance monitoring runs partly in your browser. It records page timings and crash reports — not the contents of what you type.

## 8. Children

Sideline is built for sports teams, which frequently include minors. If you are under 16, a parent or guardian must consent to your use of it.

**Team admins are responsible for obtaining that consent** before adding a minor to a team.

## 9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Anything material will also be announced in the app.
