The AI assistant is a chat panel that answers questions about your team's own data — in plain language, with links to the exact events, members, groups, rosters, and training types it's talking about. It can also answer questions about how Sideline itself works, straight from these documentation pages.

<Aside type="caution" title="It can propose changes, and only with your confirmation">
The assistant is mostly read-only. It can propose creating, changing or cancelling an event, and confirming attendance for a training, with the same permissions the event page needs. Anyone can ask it to answer an event invitation for themselves (never for someone else). With group or roster management permission it can also propose creating a group or roster and adding or removing a member. With the finance permissions it can propose recording a payment, adding an expense or moving a member to another membership plan; the card shows the exact amount, currency, member and date. With the **AI Assistant: Manage Discord Setup** permission plus group-management permission, it can also propose creating a Discord channel, changing one group's access to a channel, or archiving a channel. Each proposal shows up as a card with the details filled in, and **nothing happens until you confirm it**. You can discard the card instead. A confirmed Discord change is queued for the bot and shows up in Discord shortly. Until then, the assistant reports it as pending. It cannot make any other kind of change — use the normal event, roster, or group pages for that.
</Aside>

## Where to find it

Go to **Assistant** in your team's sidebar (`/teams/{teamId}/assistant`). The assistant is a deployment-wide setting, not a per-team one — if it hasn't been enabled on your Sideline instance, this page shows a short explanation instead of the chat box.

## What you can ask

**About your team:**

- **Events** — "What's our next event?", "Which events are cancelled this month?"
- **Training types** — "Show me our training types"
- **Members** — "How many active members do we have?"
- **Groups** and **rosters**
- **Who's coming** — "Who said yes to Thursday's training?"
- **Training attendance** — "Who was marked present last week?" Only for people who can take attendance for that training, or who can see the team finances — the same rule the attendance page uses.
- **Your own training log** — "How much have I trained this month?" Your log only; it can't read anyone else's.
- **Finances** — "Which fees are outstanding?", "What's the team's payment overview?"
- **Membership plans and seasons** — "What plans do we offer?", "When does the current season end?"
- **Your Discord server** — "Which channels does our Discord have?", "Which Discord role belongs to a group?" Only for people with the **AI Assistant: Manage Discord Setup** permission (Admin and Captain by default). It shows what Sideline last saw from the bot, so a change made in Discord a moment ago may not show yet.
- **Reading Discord messages** — "What did people say in #u12-chat today?", "Summarise the last 50 messages in Juniors". The assistant reads the messages live from Discord. Only for people with the **AI Assistant: Manage Discord Setup** permission, and only in a channel that Sideline manages or creates for a group or roster, and that you have access to through one of your groups or rosters. It reads at most 100 messages, leaves out bot messages, and does at most two reads per answer. If the Sideline bot is offline, it tells you so.
- **Answering invitations** — "Say yes to Saturday's match", "I'm coming late to Thursday's training, around 19:30". It answers for you, with the same rules as the RSVP buttons: a "coming later" or "maybe" answer needs a note, and closed RSVPs stay closed. If you can edit the event, it can also set another member's answer ("Petr told me he's coming on Thursday") — you confirm the card, and the member gets a Discord message saying you set it.
- **Changing events** — "Move Thursday's practice to 19:00", "Cancel Saturday's match", "Mark Anna present and Petr absent at today's training". Each change is a card you accept or discard. Changing a recurring event changes that one occurrence only. Attendance can be confirmed once the training has started, and the card shows the whole list that will be saved.
- **Changing groups and rosters** — "Create a U14 group", "Add Anna to U12", "Take Petr off the first team". Each change is a card you accept or discard. After you accept, the member's Discord role (or the new group's role and channel) is queued for the Sideline bot and shows in Discord shortly.
- **Recording payments and expenses** — "Anna paid 1 500 Kč for the spring fee in cash today", "Add a 1 200 Kč field rent expense for March", "Move Petr to the Pro plan". Each is a card you accept or discard, and what you accept is exactly what is recorded. Accepting the same card twice records it once.
- **Changing your Discord server** — "Create a channel called Match day, then let the U12 group see it", "Let the U14 group see #coaches", "Archive the old-trainings channel". Each change is its own card you accept or discard, so that first example gives two cards. Archiving needs an archive category set up and a channel that already exists on Discord. If the bot can't move the channel to the archive, it may delete it instead.

**About Sideline itself:**

- "How do I set up a recurring event?"
- "What's a variable symbol?"
- "How does carpooling work?"

These are answered from this documentation site, so the answers match the version of Sideline you're actually running.

<Steps>

1. Type your question in the box at the bottom of the page and press Enter (or tap **Send** on mobile).
2. The assistant thinks for a moment, then replies with a short written answer.
3. Anything the answer refers to — an event, a member, a group, a roster, a training type — appears underneath as a card you can click to open that page.

</Steps>

Ask a follow-up question the same way — the assistant remembers the conversation on the page, so you can say "and what about next week?" without repeating yourself.

<Aside>
The conversation only lives in your browser tab for as long as the page is open. It isn't saved anywhere, and reloading the page (or clicking **New chat**) clears it for good.
</Aside>

## Chat with it in Discord

You can talk to the same assistant without leaving Discord. Type **/assistant** in a text channel of your team's server, and Sideline opens a thread there just for you, with a greeting from the assistant.

- **Private** (the default) — only you, and anyone you add to the thread, can see it.
- **Public** — pick `visibility: public` and the thread is visible to everyone in the channel. Only *your* messages reach the assistant; anyone else posting in the thread is ignored.

Write your questions in the thread and the assistant replies there. It remembers the conversation in that thread, so follow-up questions work just like on the web. When it suggests a change, it posts a card with **Confirm** and **Discard** buttons — nothing changes until you confirm, and only you can press them.

The assistant acts as your Sideline account, with your permissions. So the command only works once your Discord account is linked to Sideline (sign in to Sideline with Discord — `/login` gives you a link) and you're a member of the server's team. If either isn't true, Sideline tells you privately what to do and opens no thread.

## It only shows you what you can already see

This is the rule the assistant will not bend, and it matters more now that it can reach finances and attendance.

The assistant answers using your own account's permissions — exactly the same permissions that control what you see everywhere else in the app. If you can't open the Finances page, the assistant will not tell you who owes what. If you can't see a group, its events stay invisible here too. It never shows you a member's personal contact details.

Asking the assistant is not a way around a permission you don't have: when you ask about something outside your permissions, it answers as if that data doesn't exist, the same way the rest of the app would. Questions about how Sideline works are the one exception — this documentation is public, so anyone can ask about it.

## When the assistant can't answer

Sometimes the assistant can't give you a full answer. It always tells you why, in plain language — for example, if it's temporarily unavailable, if your question needed more steps than it could work through, or if it just didn't come back with anything useful. In every case, try rephrasing your question or asking something more specific.

If you've asked a lot of questions in a short time, it asks you to wait a moment before trying again — this keeps the assistant responsive for everyone on your team.
